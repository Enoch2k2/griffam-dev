# Product brief

## Positioning

GriffamDev is the professional home of Enoch Griffith, Full Stack Software Engineer.
The site should turn technical ability into evidence a prospective client can inspect.
Draft promise: “I build web applications, custom tools, and AI-powered experiences.”
Specific services and audience wording remain subject to Enoch’s confirmation.

## Audience and tasks

| Audience | Primary task | Supporting evidence | Success |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prospective client | Decide whether Enoch can solve their problem | Services, case studies, live chess demo, working process | Submit inquiry or book consultation |
| Employer / technical reviewer | Evaluate engineering ability and background | Resume, project decisions, source links where public | Download resume or connect on LinkedIn |
| Learner | Find useful coding content | Articles, videos, practical explanations | Consume a resource; later book mentoring |
| Enoch | Maintain content and manage appointments | Custom admin tools | Publish updates and manage bookings without code edits |

## First public release: must ship

- Responsive public pages: home, services, work/case studies, about, resume, contact.
- Learn hub, blog and video publishing/display; no fabricated entries to fill grids.
- Custom admin for publishing and resume/media management, protected by real authorization.
- Guest consultation booking linked to Google Calendar, management links, confirmations and reminders.
- AI chess: legal complete games, side/difficulty selection, text chat, optional hints, graceful AI outage behavior.
- Accessible light and cyberpunk dark modes, remembered preference, keyboard navigation.
- Production readiness: errors, monitoring, backups, secret handling, dependency review and domain/HTTPS.

## After launch

Client/student dashboards, paid mentoring checkout and resources, protected courses,
project tracking, file exchange, saved chess games, voice chat, advanced chess review.
The mentoring page may explain future plans but must not accept unavailable purchases.
Do not advertise account registration until accounts have useful functionality.

## Navigation

Header: brand/home, Work, Services, Learn, About, theme control, Book a call.
Sign in appears when public accounts launch; owner access uses /admin.
Learn contains Blog and Videos; About links to Resume; Work prominently links to Chess.
Footer: Contact, Resume, LinkedIn, appropriate policies. Add other socials only when supplied.
The current skeleton’s sign-in placeholder is for development, not the final launch header.

## Homepage priorities

1. Name, exact professional title, specific value proposition; Discuss a project / View work.
2. Selected case studies; each explains a problem and Enoch’s actual contribution.
3. Prominent AI chess invitation with “Play the demo” and “How it was built.”
4. Services and a clear discovery-to-delivery process.
5. Short personal introduction and resume access.
6. Recent learning content and final contact action.

## Success signals to evaluate after launch

Qualified inquiries and completed bookings; booking abandonment; chess starts and
completed games; chat failures and cost; resume downloads; article/video engagement.
No numeric conversion targets or analytics vendor are assumed yet. Never log private
booking details or full chat messages into generic analytics events.
